Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 25 November 2026

SALVAGE ("Salvage", "we") is made by NEXORA LLC. This policy explains what the app and this website collect, and why.

Your Files Stay On Your Computer

Salvage reads drives and saves recovered files only on your own computer. File names, file contents, photos and documents are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

What We Collect

  • Account: your name, email address and password. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us.
  • Plan: your plan, its end date, and when you joined and last used the app.
  • Recovery History: for each recovery run, the date, the drive letter, the scan mode, the number and total size of files found, and how long it took. This powers My Dashboard.
  • Payments: the plan you chose, the amount and the Payment ID you send us. Payments themselves are processed by NOWPayments; we never see wallet keys or card details.
  • Support Messages: what you write to us through Contact Support.

How We Use It

To sign you in, switch on the plan you paid for, show your history and answer your messages. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Where It Is Stored

Account data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), protected by security rules so that only you and the SALVAGE administrator can read your account.

Your Choices

You can ask us to export or delete your account and its history at any time by emailing masroorshahani@gmail.com. The app also works offline on the free plan without an account connection.

Children

Salvage is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
