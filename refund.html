Refund Policy

Last Updated: 25 November 2026

Try Salvage for free first: the free Quick Scan shows exactly which files it can find before you pay anything.

7-Day Refund

If you bought a paid plan and Salvage could not recover the files you needed, email us within 7 days of payment and we will refund you in full.

How To Ask

Email masroorshahani@gmail.com or use Contact Support in My Dashboard, with your account email, your Payment ID and a short description of the drive and the files you were trying to recover.

How Refunds Are Paid

Refunds are sent in USDT to a wallet address you give us, on the same network you paid with, usually within 5 business days. The plan is switched back to Free when the refund is sent.

When Refunds Do Not Apply

  • More than 7 days after payment.
  • When the files were recovered, or the drive was fully wiped before purchase, which the free scan already shows.
  • Accounts suspended for breaking the Terms Of Service.
