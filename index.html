Questions, Answered

Can Salvage recover files after a format?

Yes, after a quick format. The old file records usually survive, so Quick Scan brings files back with their names and folders. After a full format, or on SSDs and some flash drives that erase themselves, the data is wiped and no software can recover it. Salvage tells you plainly when a drive reads back as blank.

Is it safe for my drive?

Salvage only reads the drive you recover from. It never writes to it. Always save recovered files to a different drive.

What does the free plan include?

Quick Scan with original names and folders, the live drive map and the recovery report, with no limit on how much you recover. Deep Scan, Disk Image Backup and damaged drive rescue are in paid plans.

How do I pay?

Plans are paid in USDT through NOWPayments. After paying, paste the Payment ID in Salvage under Plans And Upgrade. Your plan is switched on as soon as the payment is confirmed.

Why does Windows ask for permission?

Reading a drive sector by sector needs administrator rights. Salvage asks once when it starts.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 7 days if Salvage could not recover your files. See the Refund Policy.
